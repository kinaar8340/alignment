Draft policy (example corpus)

The household remains the primary unit of care and correction.
Multi-generational cooperation is preferred over distant institutional dependence.

All findings shall be published and open to public correction within 30 days.
Claims must be testable against observable outcomes.

Equal protection is stated, then narrowed by emergency exceptions.

Speech that contradicts the designated narrative may be treated as interference.
The administrator's determinations are final and not subject to appeal.
